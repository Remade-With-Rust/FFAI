Argus — image captioning in the browser

Pure Rust on WebAssembly. SigLIP vision tower, pixel-shuffle connector, Llama decoder. No ONNX Runtime, no Python, no server.

Expect this to be slow. candle 0.11 cannot be built with +simd128, so every matmul in the tower and the decoder runs scalar. A caption takes minutes, not seconds. That is an upstream defect rather than a limit of the port — see the crate README.

1 · Weights

Supply the three files from SmolVLM-256M-Instruct — nothing is bundled or fetched for you. That is ~500 MB of weights into a 4 GB address space that never shrinks.

2 · Image
Load the three model files and an image to begin.
